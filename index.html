Each ingredient explained individually below.

Berberine

A plant alkaloid long used in traditional practice and widely studied for its role in supporting healthy blood glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity.

2

Chromium

An essential mineral that helps the body use insulin more effectively and is commonly included in glucose-support formulas to assist normal carbohydrate metabolism.

3

Cinnamon Extract

Concentrated cinnamon bark extract, traditionally used to support healthy blood sugar levels and normal insulin response after meals.

4

Alpha-Lipoic Acid

A naturally occurring antioxidant that supports glucose uptake into cells and helps protect against oxidative stress linked to fluctuating blood sugar.

Bitter Melon

A plant used for generations in traditional wellness practice for its compounds believed to support normal blood sugar regulation.

Licorice Root Extract
